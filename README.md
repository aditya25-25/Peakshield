# PeakShield
> Mountain Emergency Intelligence & Rescue Operations Platform

PeakShield is an advanced mountain emergency intelligence platform designed for rescue authorities, government agencies, disaster response teams, tourists, and trekkers in high-risk alpine terrains.

## Features
- **Real-Time Mountain Risk Engine**: Multi-source risk fusion (weather, terrain, landslide, flood, road status).
- **Mobile-First Citizen Safety**: Instant "Am I Safe?" assessments, live alerts, safe shelter routing.
- **Rescue Command Center**: Operational situation board, live incident dispatch, verified status workflow.
- **Missing Person Intelligence**: Search priority zones, last known coordinates, terrain analysis.
- **Ask the Mountain AI**: Context-aware mountain safety intelligence assistant.
- **Emergency Situation Brief Generator**: Multi-source PDF & operational summary generation.
